About Us

Learn about our story, mission, and the people behind Ubuntu Initiative

Our Story

Kivu is one of the most conflict-affected regions in the world. While growing up there, our founder Fariji Badesile Medard witnessed firsthand how the lack of educational and digital opportunity created deep, enduring barriers for his community. When he arrived in the United States, he saw the innovations coming from Kenya, Nigeria, Rwanda - and understood the massive educational gap back home.

In high school, Fariji's teacher once asked to buy his notebook because his own teaching materials were over 15 years old. That moment revealed the truth: while others accessed scholarships and free online courses, his community was learning from outdated curricula with no internet access, no computers, and no exposure to technology.

The name "Ubuntu" comes from the African philosophy meaning "I am because we are." This belief in our shared humanity drives everything we do. We founded Ubuntu Initiative to fight the digital divide - providing digital literacy, AI awareness, and educational resources so young people can participate in the modern world, access opportunities, and build better futures for their communities.

Our Vision

A Kivu where every student, teacher, and community member has the digital skills and technology access needed to participate in the global economy and unlock life-changing opportunities.

Our Mission

To bridge the digital divide in the Kivu region through community-based digital literacy training, AI awareness workshops, and educational resources - empowering youth and educators to access scholarships, remote work, and global opportunities.

Meet Our Founders

Fariji Badesile Medard

Founder & Executive Director

Fariji Badesile Medard grew up in the conflict-affected Kivu region. After arriving in the United States, he witnessed the massive educational and digital gap facing his community back home. He founded Ubuntu Initiative to fight the digital divide - believing that when people are educated and connected, they focus on personal growth rather than hopelessness. His vision: bridge local needs with global knowledge.

Mushagalusa Rymond

Co-Founder & Field Operations Director

Mushagalusa Rymond is the co-founder of Ubuntu Initiative and leads all field operations. He executes programs on the ground, working directly with communities, schools, and families to deliver education resources and digital literacy training to those who need it most.
