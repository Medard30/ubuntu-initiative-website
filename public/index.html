Our Mission

We believe in the power of "Ubuntu" - the African philosophy meaning "I am because we are." Our mission is to bridge the digital divide in the Kivu region by providing digital literacy training, AI awareness, and educational resources to students, teachers, and communities - unlocking opportunities for higher education, scholarships, remote work, and global engagement.
