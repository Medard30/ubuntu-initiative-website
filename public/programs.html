Education Support

In our regions, students often learn from outdated materials - teachers use curricula from very long time or outdated. We work to bridge this gap by providing updated learning resources and support:

  • School supplies and updated learning materials
  • Support for teachers with modern teaching resources
  • Printed guides and educational materials in local languages
  • Follow-up resources for continued learning

Digital Literacy

Limited digital literacy prevents young people from accessing scholarships, remote work, and global opportunities. Our hands-on training program provides foundational digital skills:

  • Basic computer skills and typing
  • Internet navigation and online research
  • Email, document creation, and digital communication
  • Online safety and responsible internet use

AI Awareness for Teachers & Youth

As the world advances technologically, communities in Kivu risk being left behind. Our AI awareness program helps teachers and youth understand and leverage new technologies:

  • Understanding what AI is and how it works
  • Simplifying complex AI concepts for beginners
  • Responsible use of AI tools in education and work
  • Critical thinking about AI-generated content

MHPSS & Youth Empowerment for Refugees

This program supports refugees who fled war in South Kivu (DRC) and are now living in Burundi. We provide psychosocial support, trauma-informed care, and livelihood skills to refugee youth through a holistic, community-based approach:

  • Group and individual psychosocial support sessions
  • Trauma-informed dialogue circles and emotional regulation training
  • Safe spaces for youth expression and peer support
  • Entrepreneurship training and income-generating activities
  • Basic financial literacy and mentorship
